How to interpret the comparison

A stronger scenario has at least three retained offers with independently returned matching ISBNs, known listing binding, recent evidence, and less than a threefold price spread. Three offers do not establish three independent sellers. All scenario comparisons still require review.

Every condition and binding remains separate. “All stronger scenarios below/above” describes the available scenarios, not the actual condition of your copy. Mixed results depend on which hypothetical condition and binding applies. No aggregate replacement library value is calculated.

A large-change flag means at least one same-currency stronger scenario moves either endpoint by 50% or more, or by USD 25 or more. These are review thresholds, not approval rules. Current ranges may use older AbeBooks evidence, whereas these fresh scenarios use eBay; differences are not proof of a market-price change over time.

The 54 existing whole-batch physical tasks are carried forward from their records-only adjudication. Earlier pilot identity checks are kept separate for reconciliation, rather than automatically adding new owner tasks. Grading is deferred for every book.
